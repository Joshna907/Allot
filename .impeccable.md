## Design Context

### Users
A sender at a kitchen table or a small office desk — not a trader. They already know who gets paid. They do not want candles, signals, or a terminal. They want to say the payout book out loud and hold a receipt.

### Brand Personality
Clerical, exact, unhurried. The voice of a money-order window: short sentences, named people, numbers that add up. Confidence comes from a hash you can recompute, not from a chart.

### Aesthetic Direction
A carbon-copy remittance slip on cream paper. Ink is brown-black. The only loud mark is a rubber stamp. Light theme — this is daytime paperwork, not a night desk.

Anti-references: Binance yellow trading UI, neon dashboards, SMA overlays, glass cards, hero metrics, gradient type.

Theme: light. Display: Gloock. Body: Karla.

### Design Principles
1. The receipt is the product. Everything else exists to produce one.
2. Name the people. Never say "recipient 1".
3. Say what is real and what is not, on the paper itself.
4. One pair, three names, one schedule. No extra chrome.
5. Desktop counter only. No mobile truncation.
